Controlling a Mac from Android or iPhone: What Differs

Written by the people who make Peering. Checked against the app's own code, November 2026.

The short version. You do not need an iPhone to control a Mac. Peering's Android app pairs the same way, has the same plans and does almost everything the iPhone app does. The differences are about how each phone handles the background and a few features that arrived on iPhone first.

The same on both

  • Pairing with a six-digit code or a QR code, and no account.
  • End-to-end encryption, a direct path when possible and a relay that cannot read the session.
  • The picture, trackpad mode, direct touch and joystick, pinch zoom, quality settings.
  • Keyboard bar with Mac keys, function keys, per-app shortcuts, macros and dictation.
  • The Mac's sound, the clipboard with images, files, terminals and SSH servers.
  • Displays, apps, windows and desktops as sources; darken and lock the Mac.
  • Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode chats, change review and a To review inbox.
  • Plans: free on your own network with 30 minutes a day of relay, Pro monthly or Lifetime.

Which to use

Use the phone you already carry. The Mac does not care which one connects, and you can pair both: a family can have an Android phone and an iPad on the same Mac. The plan is bought in each store separately, so a Lifetime purchase on Google Play does not carry over to an iPhone, or the other way round.

If coding agents are why you want this, and alerts with the app fully closed matter to you, the iPhone app is ahead today. On Android, keep the session in the background and the approvals arrive in the shade; see Claude Code on Android.

Getting started

The Mac side is the same either way: download Peering for Mac, allow Screen Recording and Accessibility, and pair on your home network. The step-by-step for Android is in controlling a Mac from Android; for iPhone it is in the pairing guide.

Questions

Can I control a Mac from an Android phone instead of an iPhone?

Yes. Peering's Android app pairs with the Mac the same way as the iPhone app, with no account.

Is the Android app missing features?

A few. Alerts arrive only while a session is kept in the background, one Mac is connected at a time, and there are no widgets yet. The rest matches.

Does my Pro plan carry over between Android and iPhone?

No. Purchases are made through Google Play or the App Store and belong to that store.

Can an Android phone and an iPhone both pair with the same Mac?

Yes. A Mac accepts as many paired devices as you like.
